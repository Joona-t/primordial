This multi-service architecture review spans 15 services — Auth, Billing, Ingestion, Analytics, Notification, Search, Storage, Gateway, Scheduler, Monitoring, Compliance, Reporting, Migration, Cache, and Messaging — and documents hundreds of concrete technical findings across throughput measurements, incident-driven retry limit adjustments, cross-service security audit dependencies, storage write routing constraints, and shared infrastructure patterns.
