Cross-cutting infrastructure standards applied uniformly across all 15 services include: PII classified, encrypted at rest, and access-logged under a data governance framework with retention policies from 30 days (debug logs) to 7 years (financial records) and GDPR cross-border restrictions for EU data; an observability stack using Prometheus for metrics, Jaeger for distributed tracing, and Elasticsearch for centralized logging with PagerDuty alert routing and code-managed dashboard templates; a service mesh enforcing mTLS, circuit breaking, retry policies, and weighted canary traffic splitting; a deployment pipeline gated by unit, integration, contract, and canary stages using blue-green rollback; URL-based API versioning for major changes with header-based feature flags for minor additions and a minimum 6-month backward compatibility window after deprecation; and weekly performance testing (load, stress, soak) with quarterly capacity planning model updates.
